		\Large \textbf{Open research position (Bachelor)} 
	\end{center}
	
	The \href{https://vigneshkannan-group.github.io/vk-mxm-website/}{Mechanics and Materials under eXtremes} research group within the Laboratoire de M{\'e}canique des Solides (LMS) at the Ecole Polytechnique in Palaiseau, France, is looking for passionate \textbf{undergraduate} students to study the \textbf{dynamic behavior of materials using a micro-Kolsky bar}. The project is expected to start between \textbf{January 11-18, 2027} for a duration of \textbf{8 weeks}.

	\begin{myhlbox}{\textbf{Research topic}}
		\textbf{Context:~}The constitutive stress-strain behavior of materials are strongly dependent on the strain rate and stress state of loading. Measurement of the dynamic response, and the fundamental mechanisms governing material behavior such extreme dynamic conditions (withing time scales of microseconds) are long standing challenges in mechanics of materials.
		
		\textbf{Research project:~}The student will focus on the development of experimental protocol, high-speed optical instrumentation, and in-situ imaging for a new high-precision ``micro-'' Kolsky bar experiment developed within the group. The experiment is designed to push the maximum achievable strain rates up to $10^{5}$/s in hundreds of micrometer samples (deformation within tens of microseconds). The student will perform first experiments on copper at these very high strain rates, perform data processing to extract dynamic stress-strain data, and compare with state of the art in literature.
	
	\end{myhlbox}
	
	\begin{myhlbox}{\textbf{Your profile}}
		\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=-0.25em]

			\item Background in mechanics and/or materials (demonstrated through coursework/internships)
			\item Eager to work on experimental development including mechanical testing and instrumentation
			\item Experience with data processing tools using Python/Matlab or equivalent 
			\item Comfortable communicating in English, and enjoy working in an international environment. 
		\end{itemize}

	\begin{myhlbox}{\textbf{About our team -- \href{https://vigneshkannan-group.github.io/vk-mxm-website/}{Mechanics and Materials under eXtremes}}}
		The mXm team, led by Prof.~Vignesh Kannan, studies the mechanics of materials and structures under large stresses, multi-physical fields and short time scales. We use mechanical design, electronic and optical instrumentation to push the cutting edge of experimental mechanics down to nano-second time scales, and \textmu m length scales. Our experiments are motivated by theoretical foundations, with the goal of understanding fundamental mechanisms that govern the multi-physical response of materials. We continuously seek to develop a collaborative, supportive and international group of scientists who enjoy the pursuit of exciting problems, and each other's company!
	\end{myhlbox}
